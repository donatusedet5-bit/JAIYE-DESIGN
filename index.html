Questions founders ask

Ask yours on WhatsApp
How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, so every project starts with a free quote. Tell me what you're building and you'll get a clear price and a plan before any work starts.

How soon can you start, and how long will it take?

Message me and I'll tell you honestly when I can start and how long your project should take, once I understand the scope.

Can you work with my developers?

Yes. They get a Figma file with components, variables and specs, plus a walkthrough. I stay available to answer questions and review the build.

Do you only design screens?

No. I'm a product manager too, so I can shape the roadmap, write the PRD and set priorities, then design what we agreed.

What do I get at the end?

Every screen and component in Figma, a clickable prototype, and specs and assets for your engineers. It's all yours.

Do you work with teams outside Nigeria?

Yes. I'm based in Lagos (WAT, UTC+1) and work remotely with teams worldwide.

Can you also do motion and branding?

Yes. I design product motion and brand pieces too. You can see some in the Motion section above.
