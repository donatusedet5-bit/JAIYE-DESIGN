I design products people actually understand

I'm Jaiye Oluwatishe Daniel, a product designer and design-led product manager based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with teams anywhere in the world.

I start with the problem, not the pixels. I sketch and ideate first, wireframe second, and only then design the screens, so every decision has a reason you can see.

I've designed fintech, SaaS, healthtech, AI and Web3 products, plus the brands, websites and motion around them.

How I work

Five steps, from a real problem to screens engineers can build

01

Discover

I talk to users and read the data to find who is stuck and why, before drawing anything.

Research
  • User interviews
  • Product data and funnels
  • Competitor audit
  • Support tickets
02

Sketch

Flows and fast pencil sketches. Many ideas, cheap to throw away.

03

Wireframe

Structure and flow in grey boxes, tested with users before any colour goes in.

04

Design

High-fidelity screens on a design system, so the product stays consistent as it grows.

05

Prototype & handoff

Clickable prototypes, interactions and specs, so engineers build exactly what was designed.
