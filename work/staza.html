A marketplace that holds your money until delivery.

Role

Platform

Year
Concept case study, 2026

The problem

Shoppers pay first and hope the order arrives as described. One bad delivery and they stop buying online. Sellers lose good customers because of other sellers' bad deliveries.

What I did

  • Interviewed shoppers and sellers about trust
  • Designed Protected Pay: the money waits until the buyer confirms
  • Designed the shopper app, the seller dashboard, the admin console and the design system
