The problem

Buyers want handmade goods from several small studios across West Africa, but each studio ships separately. Delivery costs pile up and makers lose the sale at checkout.

From problem to screen

01Problem & ideation

The problem: Buyers want handmade goods from several small studios across West Africa, but each studio ships separately. Delivery costs pile up and makers lose the sale at checkout. I start with rough pencil sketches, many ideas, cheap to throw away.

02Wireframe

Model. How one parcel works: fill one bag, makers drop off at a hub, it ships together. Shopper. Home, category, product, checkout, tracking and maker shop.

03Design

Seller. Home, orders, products, add product, payouts and shop design. System. A warm, photo-led design system.
