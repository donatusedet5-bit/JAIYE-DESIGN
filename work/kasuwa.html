The problem

Buyers want handmade goods from several small studios across West Africa, but each studio ships separately. Delivery costs pile up and makers lose the sale at checkout.

What I did

  • Designed one checkout across many makers
  • Designed one-parcel delivery: everything meets at a hub and ships together
  • Designed the seller dashboard for orders, products, payouts and shop design
