The problem

Getting seen means queues, repeating your history and losing prescriptions. Patients delay care until it is urgent, and doctors start every visit blind.

From problem to screen

01Problem & ideation

The problem: Getting seen means queues, repeating your history and losing prescriptions. Patients delay care until it is urgent, and doctors start every visit blind. I start with rough pencil sketches, many ideas, cheap to throw away.

02Wireframe

Time budget. Set a two-minute budget from opening the app to a booked visit. Intake. Chips, not a form, with a safety check that diverts to urgent care.

03Design

Visit. Waiting room, video consult and prescription. Record. One history from every source, shared only with permission.

01 · Sketch
What I did
  • Designed symptom intake that takes three taps and includes a safety check
  • Matched patients to the soonest suitable doctor with the slot already picked
  • Designed the video visit, e-prescription, health record and doctor view
