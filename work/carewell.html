The problem

Getting seen means queues, repeating your history and losing prescriptions. Patients delay care until it is urgent, and doctors start every visit blind.

What I did

  • Designed symptom intake that takes three taps and includes a safety check
  • Matched patients to the soonest suitable doctor with the slot already picked
  • Designed the video visit, e-prescription, health record and doctor view
