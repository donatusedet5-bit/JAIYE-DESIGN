The problem

Across Lagos, several flats share one prepaid meter. One person buys units, then chases everyone else for money, and the compound argues about who used what.

What I did

  • Wrote an eight-page PRD: problem, users, goals and metrics, requirements, key flow, release plan and risks
  • Designed the shared wallet, the fair split and automatic top-ups
  • Designed the app screens the PRD describes
