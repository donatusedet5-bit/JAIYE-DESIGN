The problem

Self-taught learners quit when they lose the thread. Most course platforms show content, not progress, and nobody notices when a learner drifts away.

What I did

  • Designed onboarding that builds a plan from the hours the learner actually has
  • Put a small pod of six learners and one clear weekly goal at the centre
  • Designed the lesson player, schedule and week-one win, plus the design system
All 4 screens laid out like a Figma page. Drag, zoom, click one to focus.
