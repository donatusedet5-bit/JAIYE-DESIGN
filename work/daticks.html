The problem

01Sketch

The problem: Data analysts finish courses but still can't prove they are ready for the job. Certificates show attendance, not ability, so hiring managers can't tell who can actually analyse data.

02Wireframe

Discover. Interviews with analysts and hiring managers on how skills are judged today. Define. One promise: practise on real scenarios, then prove it with one exam and one report.

03Design

Design. Marketing site, learner dashboard, challenges, exam and streaks on one system. Ship. Handoff, design QA and iteration with the engineering team.

01 · Sketch
What I did
  • Ran research with 30+ analysts, learners and hiring managers to define what 'job-ready' means
  • Shaped the product: a guided 14-day track of real-scenario challenges ending in a proctored validation exam and a shareable competency report
  • Designed the marketing site, learner dashboard, daily challenge, instant feedback, exam flow, streaks, profile, settings and help centre
  • Built the design system and worked with engineers through handoff and QA
