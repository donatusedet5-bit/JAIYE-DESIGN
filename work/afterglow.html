The problem

A three-night beach festival needed one idea that works on a poster, a billboard and a phone.

What I did

  • Made the sun sit lower on each poster, so the series reads as one sunset
  • Designed the billboard and a countdown where the sun sinks as the date gets closer
  • Set the system: serif, grotesk, mono and five colours
All 2 screens laid out like a Figma page. Drag, zoom, click one to focus.
