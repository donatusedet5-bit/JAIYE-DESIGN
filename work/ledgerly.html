RoleProduct designer
PlatformiOS, Android

The problem

Freelancers paid from abroad lose money to fees they only see after the transfer, and a failed payout leaves them guessing where their money went.

From sketch to screen

01Sketch

The problem: Freelancers paid from abroad lose money to fees they only see after the transfer, and a failed payout leaves them guessing where their money went.

02Wireframe

Research. Published freelancer payment research and public complaints, mapped into a journey. Failure map. Listed every way a payout can fail and what the user sees.

03Design

Flows. KYC onboarding and withdrawal with failed-payout recovery. Design. Ledger-style screens where figures line up and nothing is hidden.

01 · Sketch
What I did
  • Listed every fee and the final amount before anyone commits
  • Designed KYC as a hub, not a tunnel: each step saves and can be left
  • Designed failed-payout recovery that names the cause and offers the fastest fix
